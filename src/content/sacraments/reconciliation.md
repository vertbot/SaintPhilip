Reconciliation is a sacrament of mercy. Sin wounds the soul as an infection wounds the body, and only God's grace can heal it. Those who receive this sacrament receive forgiveness and God's peace.

**Confession is offered every Saturday from 3:00 to 4:30 pm, or by appointment.**

## How to go to Confession

1. **Prepare.** Examine your life in light of the [Ten Commandments](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ccc_css/archive/catechism/command.htm), the [Beatitudes](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ccc_css/archive/catechism/p3s1c1a2.htm) and the example of Christ, and pray for God's forgiveness.
2. **Begin.** The priest welcomes you and you both make the Sign of the Cross. You may mention when you last went to Confession and anything that will help the priest guide you.
3. **Confess your sins.** The priest offers advice and gives you a penance, such as prayer, self-denial or a work of mercy.
4. **Pray an Act of Contrition**, expressing sorrow for your sins and your resolve not to sin again.
5. **Receive absolution.** The priest extends his hands over your head and prays the words of absolution. You answer "Amen."
6. **Go in peace.** Continue your conversion by a life renewed according to the Gospel.
