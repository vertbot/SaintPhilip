Christ promised to nourish his followers on earth and to welcome them to the heavenly banquet. The Eucharist fulfills that promise: in this sacrament Christ offers his own Body and Blood to feed us, body and soul.

## First Communion

Children in 2nd grade or above are invited to prepare for First Communion through the parish Religious Education program. For more information, call the Religious Education office at 860-429-2860.

## Eucharistic Adoration

The Blessed Sacrament is exposed for Adoration every Friday after the 8:00 am Mass, with the Chaplet of Divine Mercy at 11:50 am and Benediction at noon.

Volunteers are needed to spend an hour with the Lord. If you would like to help, [contact the parish office](/contact).
