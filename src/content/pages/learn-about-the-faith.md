## Adult Bible Study

These notes come from the courses of Ed Conlan, who taught CCD and Bible study at St. Philip and St. Jude from 1980 to 2024, and more than 60 years in all. They are shared here so parishioners can read, review and print them. More notes will be added over time.

- **The Mass: Act of Worship.** The roots of the Mass from the Jewish family meal to the Last Supper, the meaning of church customs and symbols, and a visitor's guide to the church. [Read the notes (PDF)](https://saintphilipct.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Act-of-Worship.pdf)
- **The Ten Commandments: An Overview.** Natural and revealed law, conscience, and original, mortal and venial sin. [Read the notes (PDF)](https://saintphilipct.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Ten-Commandments.pdf)
- **The Parable of the Prodigal Son.** Why Jesus taught in parables, and what this one says about forgiveness and God's love. [Read the notes (PDF)](https://saintphilipct.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Parable-The-Prodigal-Son.pdf)
- **Holy Eucharist.** The institution of the Eucharist, common questions about Communion, and the Church's teaching on the Real Presence. [Read the notes (PDF)](https://saintphilipct.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Holy-Eucharist-combined-all-pages.pdf)
- **Vestments and Sacred Objects.** The liturgical year, the priest's vestments and their prayers, and the objects used at Mass. [Read the notes (PDF)](https://saintphilipct.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Vestments-Materials-combined-all-pages.pdf)

## Becoming Catholic

Adults who are interested in becoming Catholic, or who were baptized but never confirmed, are welcome to join the Order of Christian Initiation of Adults (OCIA). [Learn more on the Baptism page](/mass-and-sacraments/baptism#becoming-catholic-as-an-adult) or [contact the parish office](/contact).
