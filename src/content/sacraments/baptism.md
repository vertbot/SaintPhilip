Baptism is the first sacrament Christians receive, a welcoming into the family of Christ. The pouring of water symbolizes the washing away of sin and the living water Christ promises to those who follow him.

## Baptism for infants

Baptism is celebrated by appointment. Parents who would like their child baptized at St. Philip must first [register with the parish](/new-here).

At least one godparent must be a confirmed Catholic and must provide a copy of their Confirmation certificate before the Baptism is scheduled.

Please call the parish office at 860-429-2860 to arrange to take part in the Baptism program.

## Becoming Catholic as an adult

The Order of Christian Initiation of Adults (OCIA, formerly RCIA) is for adults who are not baptized, or who were baptized in another Christian tradition, and who have not received instruction, First Communion or Confirmation.

Participants journey together with members of the parish toward full initiation into the Catholic faith, which is celebrated at the Easter Vigil with the Sacraments of Baptism, Confirmation and Eucharist. Weekly meetings run from September through May. To learn more, [contact the parish office](/contact).
