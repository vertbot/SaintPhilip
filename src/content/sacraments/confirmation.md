---
title: Confirmation
order: 4
summary: A two-year preparation for young people in 8th and 9th grade.
---
In Confirmation the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit are given and received, along with the anointing with fragrant chrism oil, a reminder that the Holy Spirit has conferred blessings and sacred gifts.

Candidates complete a two-year preparation: classroom instruction and spiritual formation, supported by service and a retreat. All parts must be completed before the pastor declares a candidate ready to receive the sacrament.

Confirmation classes for 8th and 9th grade meet every other Sunday from 9:50 to 10:50 am. To learn more, call the parish office at 860-429-2860.
