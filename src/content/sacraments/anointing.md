title: Anointing of the Sick
order: 6
summary: For anyone seriously ill, facing surgery, or frail with age. Emergency line available.
---
**In an emergency, call Fr. Michael on his cell phone at 860-334-5306.**

Anointing of the Sick is a sacrament of mercy and healing. It includes prayers for recovery, but above all it gives spiritual strength and peace to someone who is suffering.

It is not only for those in danger of death. Anyone with a serious or chronic illness, physical or emotional, or the frailty of old age may receive it, and those facing surgery or a hospital stay may ask for it beforehand. To arrange a time, call the parish office at 860-429-2860.

Holy Communion is brought each week to parishioners who are sick or unable to come to church. If you are in the hospital, the parish would like to visit and pray with you.
