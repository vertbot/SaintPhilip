---
title: Marriage
order: 5
summary: Couples meet with the pastor at least six months before the wedding.
---
Marriage is both a vocation, a calling from God, and a sacrament. Couples who enter into Matrimony receive special gifts and graces, intending their union to be permanent, faithful and fruitful.

The couple must meet with the pastor **at least six months before the wedding**. No date is set until after this meeting. The six months allow time for the required meetings, the pre-marriage program and preparing the celebration of the sacrament.

**To schedule a meeting with the pastor, call the parish office at 860-429-2860.**
